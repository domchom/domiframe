DomiFrame

Color e-paper photo frames

Send photos to a color e-paper frame.

DomiFrame is a picture frame with a six-color E Ink screen. Anyone with a frame's link or code can send it photos from a phone or computer. The frame checks for new pictures on a schedule, redraws, and goes back to sleep. There are no accounts.

How it works Get the iPhone app

How it works

  1. Pick photos

    Open the frame's link on a phone or computer and choose photos or a folder. Crop and adjust them, and add a caption or your name if you want.

  2. They're converted and encrypted

    Your browser dithers each photo to the panel's six inks (black, white, red, yellow, blue and green) and encrypts it with the frame's code before uploading it.

  3. The frame updates

    At its next check-in, or when you press its button, the frame downloads the picture and redraws, which takes about 20 seconds.

The frame

E Ink Spectra 6

Screens
7.3" (800 × 480) or 13.3" (1600 × 1200)
Inks
6: black, white, red, yellow, blue, green
Power
Rechargeable battery, months per charge
Screen power
None: the picture stays with the power off
Updates
Wi-Fi check-ins, on a schedule you set
Holds
Up to 200 pictures, in folders, in order or shuffled

Screen

E Ink reflects light instead of emitting it, and holds an image with no power. The frame only wakes to check for new pictures.

Encryption

Each frame makes its own code and shows it on its screen. Photos are encrypted with that code in the browser before they're uploaded, so the server only stores encrypted data and can't see the pictures.

The iPhone app

iPhone and iPad

Everything the website does, plus a few things a website can't:

  • Send from anywhere: share photos to a frame from Photos, Messages or Files.
  • Alerts: hear when a frame is late or its battery is low.
  • Widgets: what each frame is showing, on your Home Screen or Lock Screen.
  • iCloud sync: your frames on all your devices, codes end-to-end encrypted.
  • Invite links open in the app: tap a frame's link and it opens there.
