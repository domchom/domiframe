Terms of use

The short version: DomiFrame is a small, free project for sending photos to e-paper frames. Send only pictures you're allowed to share, keep your frame code safe, and keep your own copies of photos that matter.

Using DomiFrame

These terms apply to the website (domiframe.art), the DomiFrame app and the service behind them. By using them you agree to these terms. If you don't agree, please don't use them.

Your pictures

Your pictures stay yours. You give DomiFrame only the permission it needs to store them, encrypted, and deliver them to the frame. Send only pictures you have the right to share, and nothing illegal, hateful or meant to harass. Because pictures are encrypted, DomiFrame can't see or check them; the people who have a frame's code are responsible for what's on it, and any of them can remove a picture.

Frame codes

A frame's code, or its invite link, lets anyone who has it send, see and remove that frame's pictures. Share it only with people you trust. The frame can always show its code again. It can also make a new one: the pictures sealed with the old code are put away for 30 days, in case the old code is typed back into the frame, and then deleted.

Not a backup

A frame holds up to 200 pictures, and removed pictures are deleted for good after 30 days. Keep your own copies of anything you care about.
